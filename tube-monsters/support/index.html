Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Collection → Shop and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy. On a new device or after reinstalling, your purchases usually come back by themselves when the app starts.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove ads once in Collection → Shop (you can also get there from Settings → Remove ads). It turns off the full-screen ads between levels, forever. The bundle with all five Legendary monsters includes Remove ads too.

Short videos for extra hints stay available after that, but they're always optional: you only see one when you choose to.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings and tap Reset progress at the bottom, then confirm. All stars and unlocked levels are cleared. Your settings and your purchases stay.

The animations are too much. Can I calm them down?

Yes: turn on Reduce motion in Settings for calmer monsters and no confetti. By default it follows your iPhone's own Reduce Motion setting (iOS Settings → Accessibility → Motion).

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

In the app: Settings → Privacy settings (shown where your region requires a consent form). For tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
